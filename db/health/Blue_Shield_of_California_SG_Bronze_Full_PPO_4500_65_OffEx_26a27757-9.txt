It is only a summary and it is included as part of the Evidence of Coverage (EOC).1 Please read both documents carefully for details. Medical Provider Network: Full PPO Network This Plan uses a specific network of Health Care Providers, called the Full PPO provider network. Providers in this network are called Participating Providers. You pay less for Covered Services when you use a Participating Provider than when you use a Non-Participating Provider. You can find Participating Providers in this network at blueshieldca.com. Pharmacy Network: Rx Ultra Drug Formulary: Standard Formulary Calendar Year Deductibles (CYD)2 A Calendar Year Deductible (CYD) is the amount a Member pays each Calendar Year before Blue Shield pays for Covered Services under the Plan. Blue Shield pays for some Covered Services before the Calendar Year Deductible is met, as noted in the Benefits chart below.
