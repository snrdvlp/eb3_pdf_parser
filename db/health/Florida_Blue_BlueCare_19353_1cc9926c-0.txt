Your Grievance and Appeals Rights: There are agencies that can help if you have a complaint against your plan for a denial of a claim. This complaint is called a
grievance or appeal. For more information about your rights, look at the explanation of benefits you will receive for that medical claim. Your plan documents also
provide complete information to submit a claim, appeal, or a grievance for any reason to your plan. For more information about your rights, this notice, or assistance,
contact the insurer at 1-800-352-2583. You may also contact your State Department of Insurance at 1-877-693-5236 or the Department of Labor's Employee Benefits
Security Administration at 1-866-444-EBSA (3272) or www.dol.gov/ebsa/healthreform. For group health coverage subject to ERISA contact your employee services
department. For non-federal governmental group health plans and church plans that are group health plans contact your employee services department. You may
also contact the state insurance department at 1-877-693-5236. Additionally, a consumer assistance program can help you file your appeal. Contact U.S. Department
of Labor Employee Benefits Security Administration at 1-866-4-USA-DOL (866-487-2365) or www.dol.gov/ebsa/healthreform.
