A participating dentist is a general dentist or specialist who has agreed to accept negotiated fees as payment in full for covered services provided to plan members. Can I find out what my out-of-pocket expenses will be before receiving a service? Yes. You can ask for a pretreatment estimate. Your general dentist or specialist usually sends Horizon a plan for your care and requests an estimate of benefits. The estimate helps you prepare for the cost of dental services. Do I need an ID card? No. You do not need to present an ID card to confirm that you are eligible. You should notify your dentist that you are enrolled in the Horizon Healthy Smiles plan. May I choose a non-participating dentist? The Horizon Healthy Smiles plan offers coverage for in-network benefits only and choosing a non-participating dentist will result in full out-of-
pocket responsibility. How do I find a participating dentist? To locate a participating provider, please utilize the doctor finder at horizonblue.com/doctorfinder or by calling 1-800-4-Dental. Simply log in or continue as a guest, select dental, select the Horizon Healthy Smiles plan, input any location nationwide, select the dentist, specialty or group practice and the results will automatically generate based on the network(s) your plan belongs to.
